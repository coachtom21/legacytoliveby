## Architecture

**Now:** 100% testnet accrues XP proof of presence while building toward a 99% mainnet solution on **May 17, 2030**.

**Detente moment (May 17, 2030):** 99% of the proven framework may become the mainnet solution. **1% stays permanently in testnet** as pure human behavior — the part no financial system, algorithm, or institution can own, settle, or extinguish.

**Next cycle:** That permanent 1% testnet seeds the next ten-year observation window, culminating in the **May 17, 2040** Detente moment.

```
Pre-genesis (now)     → 100% testnet · XP presence accrues
May 17, 2030          → 99% operational lessons → mainnet
                        1% permanent human-behavior testnet (never migrated)
2030–2040             → 10-year control-group / observation cycle
May 17, 2040          → Next Detente moment
```

## Non-negotiable rules (Utsav + Cursor)

1. **Testnet XP is never extinguished.**
2. **Testnet history is never** transferred out, deleted, spent, redeemed, or reset.
3. **May 16 reconciliation** produces a verified snapshot or testament — an interpretation of evidence, not a terminal disposition.
4. **May 17 mainnet** may reference 99% of the proven testnet framework; it does **not consume** the underlying XP presence record.
5. The **permanent 1% testnet** remains open for gratitude, presence, rejection, return, uncertainty, and other human behavior that cannot be reduced to financial settlement.
6. **Never merge** the persistent presence record with extinguishable financial obligations.

### Standing Cursor rule

> Testnet XP represents immutable proof of human presence and must never be extinguished, redeemed, transferred, spent, or deleted. Reconciliation creates a dated interpretation of the evidence, not a terminal disposition. On May 17, 2030, mainnet may adopt the operational lessons represented by 99% of the testnet solution, while at least 1% remains an independent human-behavior testnet and begins the next ten-year cycle. Never merge the persistent presence record with extinguishable financial obligations.

## What may be extinguished?

| Record layer | May be extinguished? | Treatment |
|--------------|---------------------|-----------|
| XP proof of presence | **No** | Permanently preserved |
| Consent and behavioral event | **No** | Append-only evidence |
| Testnet recognition | **No** | Continues across cycles |
| Fiat-side obligation | Potentially | Governed outside the XP ledger |
| Mainnet settlement entry | Potentially | Separate accounting rules |
| Corrected event | Original remains | New correction event appended |

If `EXTINGUISHED` remains in the schema for pledge or mainnet accounting, it must be **isolated from testnet XP presence records**.

## Testnet XP lifecycle (corrected)

**Do not use** `issued → pending → matured → disputed → extinguished` for testnet XP.

**Use instead:**

```
Observed → Confirmed → Reconciled → Recognized → Carried Forward
```

- Disputes may **qualify** evidence; they cannot **erase** the fact that the human interaction occurred.
- Corrections append new events; originals are never mutated.

---

## Relationship to mainnet obligation states

The mentorship thread’s obligation state machine (`issued`, `pending`, `matured`, `disputed`, `extinguished`) applies to **fiat-side obligations and mainnet settlement entries** — not to testnet XP presence.

| Layer | State machine |
|-------|----------------|
| Testnet XP presence | Observed → Confirmed → Reconciled → Recognized → Carried Forward |
| Fiat / mainnet obligations | Issued → Pending → Matured / Disputed → Extinguished (where applicable) |
